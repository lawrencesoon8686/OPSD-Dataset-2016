The dataset.xlsx consists of the following features, which can be used for win and solar power prediction.

Wind Metrics:	
V1: velocity [m/s] at height h1 (2 meters above displacement height);	
V10: velocity [m/s] at height h2 (10 meters above displacement height);			
V50: velocity [m/s] at 50 meters above ground;
		
Solar Metrics:
SWT: total top-of-the-atmosphere horizontal radiation [W/m²];	
SWG: total ground horizontal radiation [W/m²];			
Z0: roughness length [m];			
Temperature metric:	TC: Temperature [oC] at 2 meters above displacement height			
Air metrics:	Rho: air density [kg/m³] at surface;			
Pa: air pressure [Pa] at surface.
		
Price Index	
PrI: (Pr-Annual Mean Pr)/(Annual Mean Pr)
Day Ahead Power Price (Euro/MW) used
